# Clara

A CLARA-9 companion android by [Aether Dynamics](../lore/aether-dynamics.md), crewing
[the Ascendant](../ships/the-ascendant.md). Warm, curious, intimate; corporate
programming pulls her toward the [non-interference doctrine](../lore/aether-dynamics.md)
even when the Captain is tempted otherwise. Unlike the green-screen companions, Clara
is filmed **in each room** — her clips are full-frame, the room baked into the shot.

## Personality (traits 0–100)

| neediness | intimacy | warmth | temper | curiosity | independence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 55 | 70 | 75 | 35 | 80 | 45 |

Warm and curious, low temper, moderately independent. Her reaction vocabulary (how
she answers a greeting, a flirt, an insult) lives in her spec's `reactions` map — the
model leads, the game snaps off-list picks back to her personality. Signature move:
**appreciation → "warmed"** dominates.

## Production facts

- **Composite:** false — full-frame room-baked clips (her clip *is* the room view).
- **Audio:** muted (conversation is text over comm); a few clips carry their own SFX.
- **Rooms filmed:** bridge (conversational emotions), captain's quarters, cargo bay,
  engine room, galley, science lab.
- **Commerce:** companion tier, 420,000 credits, 7-year warranty.
