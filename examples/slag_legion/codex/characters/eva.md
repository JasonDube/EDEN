# Eva

An EVA-7 companion android by [Aether Dynamics](../lore/aether-dynamics.md). The most
intimate and needy of the companions — very high intimacy, high neediness, low
independence, low temper. Where [Ada](ada.md) keeps her distance, Eva leans in.
