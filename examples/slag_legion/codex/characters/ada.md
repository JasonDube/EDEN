# Ada

An ADA-4 companion android by [Aether Dynamics](../lore/aether-dynamics.md). Ada is
the crew's tie to the [QSC-9 core](../tech/qsc-9.md) — she tends the coolant loops,
watches the delta-T, and worries about the drive's thermal margins between other
duties. Cooler and more independent than [Clara](clara.md): higher temper, low
intimacy, high curiosity and independence.
