# Akelba

The first world of the game — the planet you can fly down to and walk. Conceived as
"a planet in and of itself": in the space game you land on various planets and
celestial bodies, and akelba is the first of its class. It began hand-sculpted, and
its generation recipe became the template every seeded planet now inherits.

## As built (engine)

Akelba is an **akelba-class wrappable world**: a 32×32-chunk terrain (~4032 units ≈
2.5 miles square) that **wraps at the edges** — walk off one side, arrive at the
other. Rolling, low-frequency terrain (not spiky), generated by seeded FBM noise;
the wrap-edge flattening smooths the seam into gentle plains.

- Terrain: temperate, rolling hills (~75 base habitability).
- Wrappable planet illusion (flat-world torus), the classic walkable-planet approach.
- Template: **New Level → "World (2.5 mi wrappable planet)"** in TED, with a seed
  knob — every seed is a different planet from akelba's generator. A celestial body =
  this config + a seed.

## Roadmap

The next horizon for planets is the **spherified cube** (6 square faces bent onto a
sphere) for true curvature, a visible horizon, and circumnavigation from orbit — see
[universe roadmap](../design/universe-roadmap.md). Akelba is the proving ground.
