# Rhovani

![Rhovani](../../assets/species/rhovani.jpg)

**Physical.** Graceful humanoids with tawny fur covering their bodies and tall pointed ears. Feline facial features with whiskers. Retractable claws.

**Culture.** Tribal monarchy where the strongest bloodline rules. Sacred coronation rituals at the great standing stones. Royal hunts determine succession disputes.

**Homeworld.** Rolling grasslands with ancient stone circles and riverside villages

**Building style.** Thatched longhouses with carved totems, a royal hall of stacked stone and animal hides
