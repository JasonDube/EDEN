# Kethari League

![Kethari League](../../assets/species/kethari_league.jpg)

**Physical.** Lithe humanoids with dark blue skin and a crest of sensory quills running from forehead to nape. Double-jointed limbs give them unusual agility.

**Culture.** Coastal city-states united by a trade league. Each city governs itself but sends representatives to a rotating capital for league matters.

**Homeworld.** Archipelago with hundreds of inhabited islands

**Building style.** Whitewashed stone port cities with shared lighthouse networks and iron-chain harbors
