# Krennick Industrial Group

![Krennick Industrial Group](../../assets/species/krennick_industrial_group.jpg)

**Physical.** Stout humanoids with sallow skin and dark circles under their eyes from long work hours. Calloused hands. Company badges permanently affixed to clothing.

**Culture.** Mega-corporations own entire cities. Workers live in company housing, shop at company stores. Board of Directors serves as government. Child labor common.

**Homeworld.** Fully industrialized with company-owned districts replacing traditional cities

**Building style.** Factory-cities with company housing blocks, executive mansions on hilltops, rail networks branded by corporation
