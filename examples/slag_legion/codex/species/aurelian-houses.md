# Aurelian Houses

![Aurelian Houses](../../assets/species/aurelian_houses.jpg)

**Physical.** Elegant humanoids with marble-white skin and ice-blue eyes. Thin lips and high cheekbones. Elongated fingers weighted with iron signet rings.

**Culture.** Five noble houses control iron mines, ports, farmland, armies, and the priesthood. Senate exists but only oligarchs may hold seats. Lavish banquets while peasants starve.

**Homeworld.** Coastal empire with each house controlling a vital resource or institution

**Building style.** Marble villas with iron gates, private bath complexes, and impoverished slums just outside the walls
