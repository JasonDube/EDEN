# Ironbound Legion

![Ironbound Legion](../../assets/species/ironbound_legion.jpg)

**Physical.** Disciplined humanoids with gunmetal-grey skin and close-cropped hair. Uniform builds from standardized training. Rank brands on shoulders.

**Culture.** Military dictatorship with strict chain of command. Every citizen is a soldier first. Iron discipline and engineering — roads, forts, siege engines.

**Homeworld.** Fortified heartland with military roads extending in all directions

**Building style.** Standardized military camps that become permanent forts, barracks-cities, weapons foundries
