# Syndari Union

![Syndari Union](../../assets/species/syndari_union.jpg)

**Physical.** Tall lanky humanoids with ashen grey skin and oversized hands. Long flexible necks and narrow faces with vertical-slit pupils. Natural engineers.

**Culture.** Industrial federation of factory-states sharing rail networks and telegraph lines. Representative assembly meets in a grand iron rotunda.

**Homeworld.** Flat industrial heartland criss-crossed by railways

**Building style.** Iron-framed assembly halls connected by rail stations, shared telegraph offices in every town
