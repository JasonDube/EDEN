# Gilded Compact

![Gilded Compact](../../assets/species/gilded_compact.jpg)

**Physical.** Plump well-fed humanoids with rosy skin and multiple chins. Small sharp eyes behind jeweled monocles. Rings on every finger. Perfumed.

**Culture.** Banking families control credit across kingdoms. Debt is power. They don't rule openly but every king owes them. Lavish private courts rival royal ones.

**Homeworld.** Wealthy city-state where banking families live in hilltop palaces overlooking crowded lower districts

**Building style.** Gilded palace-banks on hilltops, vaulted treasure halls, ornate private chapels, squalid lower quarters
