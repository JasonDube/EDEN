# Drifter Collectives

![Drifter Collectives](../../assets/species/drifter_collectives.jpg)

**Physical.** Lanky humanoids with weather-beaten tan skin and mismatched eyes. Clothing cobbled from many cultures. Perpetual travelers with calloused feet.

**Culture.** Nomadic bands of artisans, tinkers, and free-thinkers rejecting feudal order. Traveling markets and underground printing presses spread subversive ideas.

**Homeworld.** No homeland — caravans travel between the kingdoms of other species

**Building style.** Painted wagons, temporary market stalls, hidden meeting halls in sympathetic cities
