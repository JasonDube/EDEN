# Radiant Orthodoxy

![Radiant Orthodoxy](../../assets/species/radiant_orthodoxy.jpg)

**Physical.** Pale luminous humanoids with translucent skin showing faint vein patterns. Large earnest eyes. High foreheads. Ritual robes over industrial workwear.

**Culture.** Industrial theocracy where steam power is viewed as divine gift. Factories are consecrated as temples of industry. Science is theology.

**Homeworld.** Industrial nation where every factory has a chapel and every engineer is ordained

**Building style.** Cathedral-factories with stained glass and smokestacks, steam-organ chapels, consecrated railway stations
