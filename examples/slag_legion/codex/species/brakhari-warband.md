# Brakhari Warband

![Brakhari Warband](../../assets/species/brakhari_warband.jpg)

**Physical.** Massive heavily muscled humanoids with scarred dark red skin. Prominent brow ridges and jutting jaws. Ritual battle tattoos cover every surface.

**Culture.** Warlord rules through strength of arms. Bronze weapons are sacred objects passed through victory. Children begin combat training at age four.

**Homeworld.** Harsh volcanic coastline where only the strong survive

**Building style.** Hilltop fortresses of piled stone, sparring grounds, weapon forges, and trophy halls of conquered enemies
