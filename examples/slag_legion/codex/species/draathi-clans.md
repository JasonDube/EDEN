# Draathi Clans

![Draathi Clans](../../assets/species/draathi_clans.jpg)

**Physical.** Stocky quadrupedal beings with thick grey hide and two manipulator arms growing from their shoulders. Tusked faces with small keen eyes.

**Culture.** Nomadic clans that gather seasonally at sacred meeting grounds to settle disputes and trade. Each clan maintains autonomy but swears mutual defense pacts.

**Homeworld.** Vast steppes with scattered oasis settlements

**Building style.** Portable hide-and-bone yurts arranged in clan circles, with a permanent stone meeting hall at the gathering ground
