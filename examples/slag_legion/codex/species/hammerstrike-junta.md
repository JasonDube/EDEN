# Hammerstrike Junta

![Hammerstrike Junta](../../assets/species/hammerstrike_junta.jpg)

**Culture.** Military-industrial complex where generals own factories. Ironclad warships and artillery dominate. Conscription is universal. War is the economy.

**Homeworld.** Heavily industrialized military state with fortified borders and munitions factories
