# Orenthi

![Orenthi](../../assets/species/orenthi.jpg)

**Physical.** Slender humanoids with sand-colored skin and large reflective eyes. Long pointed ears and thin fingers. Naturally quiet and observant.

**Culture.** Early republic where landowners elect a senate of elders. Written legal codes carved on bronze tablets in the central forum.

**Homeworld.** Arid river valley with irrigated farmland

**Building style.** Mudbrick administrative buildings with bronze-tablet archives and irrigation canal networks
