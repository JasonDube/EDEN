# Grubellian Merchants

![Grubellian Merchants](../../assets/species/grubellian_merchants.jpg)

**Physical.** Small rotund humanoids with mottled yellow-brown skin and beady black eyes. Multiple chins. Nimble fingers constantly counting or weighing.

**Culture.** Merchant clans run everything — the "king" is whoever controls the most trade routes. Bronze coins minted by clan patriarchs. Everything has a price.

**Homeworld.** Crossroads trading hub between three larger civilizations

**Building style.** Sprawling bazaars, fortified warehouses, and merchant-clan compounds with counting houses
