# The Ascendant

The Captain's ship — the vessel the player lives aboard between worlds. Aether
Dynamics-built, driven by a [QSC-9 Quantum Singularity Core](../tech/qsc-9.md), and
crewed by the companion androids ([Clara](../characters/clara.md),
[Ada](../characters/ada.md), [Eva](../characters/eva.md)). It is the hub the game
returns to between planetfalls on worlds like [akelba](../worlds/akelba.md).

## Interior — the walkable scenes

Authored rooms under `../../assets/scenes/`:

| Scene | Role |
|---|---|
| Bridge | Command; the companion's default station, conversation hub. |
| Engine Room | The [QSC-9](../tech/qsc-9.md) tower — thermal management, drive scheduling. |
| Captain's Quarters | The Captain's private space. |
| Cargo Bay (port / starboard) | Trade goods, salvage, storage. |
| Science Lab | Scans, archive lookups, research. |
| Galley | Crew common space. |
| Hallway | Connective spine between rooms. |

  androids' defects — but not combat or vacuum damage. What the Captain does with the
