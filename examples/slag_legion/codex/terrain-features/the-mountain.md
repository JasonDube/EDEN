# The Pink-Pool Mountain

A dramatic mountain massif — a sharp central spire ringed by lesser peaks and dark
rock, with pink mineral pools pooling in the flats around its base. Placed as a GLB
landmark on [akelba](../worlds/akelba.md); its pink-and-charcoal palette set the tone
that the planet's own terrain now echoes.

> This entry doubles as the **terrain-feature template example**: a landmark is a
> placed model (GLB), sited on a world, at some scale. Big set-pieces belong here so
> the codex tracks the *places* of the universe, not just its peoples and powers.

## As built

- Placed as a scene object in TED and walked in play mode.
- **Note (engine):** large GLBs like this exposed the play-mode "battle-fog" cull —
  now size-aware so mountain-scale models don't vanish up close.
